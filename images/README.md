## Photography Guidelines (from Brand Guidelines)

### Interior Photography
- Capture natural light flooding through windows
- Show lived-in warmth with thoughtful styling (an open book, fresh flowers, coffee cup)
- Highlight texture and materials: wood, linens, ceramics
- Use warm color temperature to enhance cozy atmosphere
- Avoid sterile, overly staged hotel photography

### Location & Heritage Photography
- Showcase the Renaissance architecture of Zamość Old Town
- Capture the Great Market Square (Rynek Wielki) and Town Hall
- Include golden hour and blue hour shots for atmosphere
- Show the walkable, human-scale nature of the UNESCO old town
- Feature arcaded merchant houses and Renaissance details

### Color Grading
- Apply warm tones that complement the brand's gold palette
- Slight lift to shadows, soft contrast
- Rich but not oversaturated colors
- Avoid cool, clinical color grades
