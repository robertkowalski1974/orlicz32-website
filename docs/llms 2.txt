## About Zamość
Zamość was founded in 1580 by Chancellor Jan Zamoyski and designed by Italian architect Bernardo Morando as an "ideal city" based on Renaissance urban planning theories. It received UNESCO World Heritage status in 1992. Key attractions include the Great Market Square (one of the most beautiful Renaissance squares in Europe), Renaissance Town Hall, Collegiate Church, Zamoyski Palace, 17th-century Synagogue, and well-preserved fortifications with bastions.
