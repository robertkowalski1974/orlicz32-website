Location

Getting Here

From Warsaw (approx. 300 km):
Take the S17 expressway towards Lublin, then continue on route 17 to Zamość. Journey time approximately 3.5-4 hours.

From Lublin (approx. 90 km):
Follow route 17/74 southeast to Zamość. Journey time approximately 1-1.5 hours.

From Kraków (approx. 300 km):
Take the A4 motorway east, then route 94 via Rzeszów and route 77 north. Journey time approximately 4-4.5 hours.

Nearest Airports:
Lublin Airport (LUZ) - approximately 100 km, with connections to Warsaw, London, and other European cities.

Rzeszów-Jasionka Airport (RZE) - approximately 120 km, with connections to major European destinations.

Parking:
Free street parking is available near our apartments. We’ll provide detailed parking instructions upon booking.

Help

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in is between 16:00 and 21:00. Check-out is before 11:00. If you need different times, please contact us in advance and we’ll do our best to accommodate you.

Is parking available?
Yes, we offer free on-premises parking supervised by CCTV.

Are pets allowed?
Unfortunately, pets are not allowed in our apartments.

Do you offer airport transfers?
We can arrange private transfers from Lublin Airport or other locations. Please contact us for pricing and availability.

What’s included in the apartment?
All our apartments include bed linens, towels, toiletries, a fully equipped kitchen, high-speed WiFi, Smart TV, and climate control. We also provide a welcome guide with local recommendations.

Can I cancel my reservation?
Our standard policy allows free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Please check the specific terms when booking.

Is the Old Town walkable from Orlicz 32?
Absolutely! We’re located just a short walk from the Great Market Square and all major attractions. You won’t need a car to explore the city.
