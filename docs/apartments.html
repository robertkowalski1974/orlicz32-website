The Apartments

Two apartments on one quiet villa street

Straightforward comfort

Two apartments, one house

Both apartments are in our own house at Orlicz-Dreszera 32, on a villa street where the loudest thing after ten at night is usually the wind. Rynek Wielki is a ten to fifteen minute walk. The car stays behind the gate, in the yard, under CCTV, and there is an EV charger about 200 m away.

We did the renovation ourselves, room by room, and we equipped both places for people staying longer than one night: a kitchen you can genuinely cook in, a dishwasher, a washing machine, fibre WiFi that holds a video call, and a desk if you have to work. Room Nr 1 takes five people, Room Nr 3 takes three.
