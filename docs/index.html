Our Philosophy

Welcome to Orlicz 32

Apartamenty Orlicz 32 is a boutique accommodation nestled in the heart of Zamość, a city recognized by UNESCO as an outstanding example of a late 16th-century Renaissance planned town.

Named after its location on Orlicz-Dreszera Street, we offer guests a unique opportunity to experience the charm of this historic “Pearl of the Renaissance” while enjoying all the comforts of a modern home.

Our meticulously designed apartments combine contemporary comfort with the timeless elegance that defines our host city. We provide personalized services and thoughtfully curated spaces that offer a true escape from the everyday hustle.

Discover

Pearl of the Renaissance

Zamość was founded in 1580 by Chancellor Jan Zamoyski and designed by Italian architect Bernardo Morando as an “ideal city” based on Italian Renaissance theories.

The city’s perfectly preserved architecture, Renaissance town square, and historical significance earned it UNESCO World Heritage status in 1992.
